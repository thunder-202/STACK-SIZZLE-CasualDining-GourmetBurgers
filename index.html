Our Story · Born in Brisbane

Good Food. Great Company.

STACK & SIZZLE started with one stubborn rule: casual food shouldn't taste like an afterthought. We fired up our first flat-top grill in Brisbane to bring proper kitchen craft—seared crusts, real cheddar melt, and scratch-made sauces—to an easygoing neighbourhood table.

Every burger begins with freshly ground Queensland pasture-raised beef pressed hard onto seasoned steel the moment your ticket prints. Our potato brioche buns arrive warm from a local bakery each morning, and our pickles, smoky sizzle sauce, and hickory glazes are prepped in-house before the doors swing open.

Whether you're dropping in after a walk along the river, splitting truffle fries with mates on a Friday night, or grabbing a thickshake for the trip home, you'll always find loud flavour and a warm welcome.

01. Pressed Fresh to Order

Never held in warming trays—every patty hits hot steel when you order.

02. Local Queensland Produce

Scenic Rim greens, Maleny dairy shakes, and locally baked potato buns.

03. Scratch-Whipped Sauces

Seven signature dips and glazes blended daily in our South Bank kitchen.
